# NieR: Automata — Opera GX Mod

**Bring the atmosphere of NieR: Automata to your browser.**

An unofficial, fan-made Opera GX mod featuring 2B, animated visuals, and music from the world of *NieR: Automata*.

[**Install from GX Store**](https://store.gx.me/ru/mods/9r58tz/nier-automata/)

<sub>Created by <strong>OcelotikiKo Studio</strong></sub>

</div>

## Features

- **2B animated wallpaper** — a moving background inspired by *NieR: Automata*.
- **“Rays of Light”** — background music for your browser.
- **NieR: Automata menu theme** — additional music from the game's atmosphere.
- **Keyboard sounds** — planned and currently in development.

> **Note:** Keyboard sounds are a work in progress and may not be available in the current release.

## Installation

1. Open **[NieR: Automata on GX Store](https://store.gx.me/ru/mods/9r58tz/nier-automata/)** in Opera GX.
2. Select **Install Mod**.
3. Open the browser's **Mods** section to manage or enable the mod's features.

The GX Store listing includes **Desktop** and **Mobile** support. Individual effects may vary by platform.
